# Punctuation

Canonical: [Commas](https://developers.google.com/style/commas),
[Hyphens](https://developers.google.com/style/hyphens),
[Quotation marks](https://developers.google.com/style/quotation-marks),
[Periods](https://developers.google.com/style/periods),
[Text-formatting summary](https://developers.google.com/style/text-formatting).

US English punctuation. Serial (Oxford) comma always.

## Commas

- Serial comma: `zones, regions, and multi-regions`.
- After introductory words and phrases (`Finally, …`).
- Between independent clauses joined by a coordinating conjunction, unless
  both clauses are very short (`Type your ID and click OK`).
- Before `which` in a nonrestrictive clause. `that` for restrictive clauses.
- Semicolon or period before conjunctive adverbs (`however`, `therefore`),
  comma after.
- Don't put a comma before `because` unless the clause is nonrestrictive.

## Periods and other end marks

- End complete sentences with a period. Questions get a question mark.
- Don't end headings with periods.
- Periods go inside quotation marks, except when the quotes wrap a literal
  string or keyword — then keep the literal exact and put other punctuation
  outside.
- Avoid exclamation points in conceptual, reference, and most procedural docs.
  See `references/voice-and-tone.md`.
- Don't use periods in acronyms (`US`, not `U.S.`).

## Quotation marks

- Straight double quotes and apostrophes, never curly.
- Technical prose barely uses quotes. Prefer code font for strings and
  keywords. Quotes are for titles of short works (when not linked), direct
  citations, and rare metaphorical uses.
- Single quotes only in code (when the language requires them) or nested
  quotations.

Recommended: The section's title is "Care and feeding of the emu."

Not recommended: The section’s title is “Care and feeding of the emu.”

## Hyphens and dashes

- Hyphen for clarity, not as a dash. Don't use `-` or `--` for an em dash.
- Prefixes: usually closed (`metadata`, `preprocessing`). Hyphenate `self-`
  and `cross-`, before a capital or number (`non-Google`, `post-2000`), and
  to avoid misreading (`re-sign`).
- Compound modifiers before a noun: hyphenate when needed
  (`a well-designed app`, `a 64-bit system`). Not after `-ly` adverbs
  (`publicly available`).
- After a verb, usually open (`The app is well designed`) unless the term is
  always hyphenated (`on-premises`, `add-on`).
- Number ranges: hyphen, not en dash (`8-20 files`). Or `from 8 to 20`.
  Don't mix (`from 8-20` is wrong).
- No spaces around hyphens except after a suspended hyphen
  (`one- or two-hour intervals`).

## Ampersands

Don't use `&` for `and` in headings, body text, navigation, or TOCs. OK when
quoting a UI label that uses `&`, in tight table headings, and in code.

## Slides for agents

When in doubt: serial comma, sentence-ending period, straight quotes, hyphen
not dash, `and` not `&`.
